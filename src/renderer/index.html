Short name

Attendants scan the QR code, enter the PIN, and tap + for each person in Left, Center and Right. The links use this computer's address on the Wi-Fi, which can change from week to week, so scan the QR code again if a saved link stops working. The short name works on iPhones and PCs but not on many Android phones.

OBS dock

Dock address
  1. In OBS: View → Docks → Custom Browser Docks…
  2. Dock Name: Attendance. URL: the dock address above. Apply.
  3. Near the end of the meeting, type the Zoom number and press Finalize.

Attendance video

In M³: Settings → Integrations → Shared folder, turn on "Add media from a shared folder" and choose this same folder. The video then appears in that meeting's media list by itself. Without it, the video is saved to the Desktop.
