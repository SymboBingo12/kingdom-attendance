Kingdom Hall Attendance

Count meeting attendance on phones, then show it as a short video on the hall monitor and on Zoom.

Kingdom Hall Attendance is a free, open-source Windows app for a Kingdom Hall's AV desk. Attendants mark each person present at the Kingdom Hall or on Zoom from their own phones, and the AV desk finalizes the count into a 20-second attendance video.

Google Sheets (optional)

If the congregation connects a Google account, the app writes each finalized meeting to two Google Sheets in that account: Attendance | Congregation name and Log for Attendance | Congregation name. It asks only for permission to work with the spreadsheets it creates itself, not the rest of the Drive.
